Optional camera · video only · no microphone

After you agree, your browser asks for camera permission. Hand detection runs on your device. Camera images and landmarks are not saved or uploaded by this game.

Enabling it downloads a pinned MediaPipe runtime from jsDelivr and a model from Google. Those hosts receive normal download metadata. This dependency has not been independently audited.

Stop camera, leaving a tab, or exiting releases the webcam. Research contribution is not enabled.

You can play the entire game without a camera.

A different kind of task director.

The local director already reads the scene, protects belongings and verifies tasks. An optional server-side model can remix the story and task order within those same rules.

Only the authored virtual scene, its IDs, ownership labels and task contract are sent to your configured server and AI provider. Never webcam frames or hand landmarks. This resets the current shift after you accept a validated remix.

Local development server required. No API key belongs in this page.

LOCAL DATA STUDIO

Record a virtual episode.

Save the rendered game camera, aligned depth and object labels, robot/object states and requested actions. This does not record your webcam, microphone or raw hand landmarks. Nothing is uploaded.

Know what you are collecting

Hotel pickup/place contacts are symbolic task relations, not physical contacts. Forces, tactile signals, joints and torques are unavailable and exported as null. Room / Overview images are privileged views; Robot camera is a virtual head view. No camera noise or physical calibration is modeled.

Images: 480 pixels wide, requested 2 Hz. Limit: 60 active simulation seconds, 120 frames or 96 MiB. Timing is recorded per sample; this is not a fixed-rate physical sensor.

No recording. Recording permission is separate from webcam permission.

ANALYTIC CONTACT FIXTURE / NOT HOTEL PHYSICS

Feel the contact.

Compress two virtual pads against a fixed block. Force follows a unilateral spring–damper model. The pressure footprint is an assumed Gaussian, not a real tactile sensor or a measured hotel grasp.
